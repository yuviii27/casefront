Questions firms ask us

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It collects information, answers logistical questions and books consultations — nothing more. Anything requiring legal judgment is routed to a human. This is the single most important design rule in everything we build.

Where does our client data live?

In your existing systems. We connect to your case management software and CRM rather than copying data into a new platform. We sign an NDA and a data processing agreement before any work begins.

What if the automation breaks?

Every workflow has error handling and alerting. If a run fails, you and we both get notified, and the retainer covers the fix. Most failures are third-party API changes, which we monitor for.

Do we own the workflows if we stop paying?

Yes. The workflows are built in your accounts and exported to you in documentation at handover. You are never renting your own business processes back from us.

How long until it's live?

About two weeks for a standard build, assuming access to your calendar and phone system in the first few days.
